History

Python was created in 1980s by Guido van Rossum. During his research at the National Research Institute for Mathematics and Computer Science in the Netherlands

The first version was released in 1991.

After gaining popularity among scientists for numerical computations and data analysis, in 1994, Python 1.0 was released

    Python 1.5 released in 1997
    Python 2.0 released in 2000
    Python 3.0 in 2008 brought newer functionalities
